## 👋 About Me

I am a **Senior Data Analyst @ Capital One**, specializing in transforming complex, large-scale datasets into production-grade ML solutions and actionable business intelligence. Bridging biotechnology engineering, product analytics, and computer science, I focus on building resilient pipelines and models at the intersection of business strategy and high-performance engineering.

* **🎯 Long-term Goal:** Transitioning to Staff AI Engineer (L6) role, focused on distributed training, large-scale systems design, and publishing industry-aligned AI research.
